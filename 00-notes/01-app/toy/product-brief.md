# SketchSpark — Product Brief

## Vision

SketchSpark turns rough sketches into polished UI designs in seconds. One sketch in, five high-fidelity options out.

## Problem

The gap between idea and testable prototype is the slowest part of the design process. Turning a napkin sketch into something a team can evaluate takes hours or days. Generating multiple directions multiplies that cost — so most teams explore one direction and hope it's right.

## Solution

SketchSpark accepts a rough sketch (photographed, drawn in-app, or imported) and generates up to five distinct UI interpretations simultaneously. Each option applies a different layout strategy while respecting the designer's spatial intent. The designer compares, selects, refines, and exports — compressing days of work into minutes.

## Core Workflow

1. **Input** — Upload a photo, draw freehand, or import from Figma/Sketch/SVG
2. **Generate** — AI produces 5 layout options in 15–45 seconds
3. **Compare** — Side-by-side review with confidence scores and annotations
4. **Refine** — Iterate on the selected direction with AI-assisted adjustments
5. **Export** — Output to Figma, SVG, PNG, HTML/CSS, or React components

## Key Differentiators

- **Sketch-first**: Preserves spatial intent that text prompts cannot capture
- **Parallel options**: Five directions from one input — more exploration, less time
- **Design token integration**: Brand-consistent output from day one
- **Accessibility by default**: WCAG AA compliance built into every generation
- **Platform-aware**: Web, iOS, and Android conventions respected automatically

## Target Users

- Solo designers accelerating client work
- Design teams exploring directions collaboratively
- Product teams bridging sketch-to-development handoff

## Success Metrics

- Time from sketch to first testable mockup: < 2 minutes
- Options generated per session: 5 (default)
- Designer satisfaction with at least one option: > 80%
- Accessibility compliance rate: 100% WCAG AA on generated output
